About Be HERE

Be HERE is a hyperlocal web platform dedicated to preserving collective memory, everyday stories, and the living atmosphere of cities and neighborhoods. Everyday snapshots of quiet streets, local bakeries, and park benches become invaluable historical documents decades later.
